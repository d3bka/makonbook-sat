FAQ

Questions users ask before signing up.

Is this for self-study or classrooms?

Both. Students can practice alone, and teachers can manage classrooms, track progress, and review performance.

Does it focus only on full tests?

No. It also includes vocabulary work, analytics, review flow, and progress tracking around test performance.

Can unfinished tests be continued later?

Yes. The platform supports resuming active attempts instead of forcing students to restart from zero.

Does it work on mobile?

The landing page is responsive. The test-taking experience should still be reviewed on actual devices before launch.
